7 · If something looks wrong

• Balance zero or slow — wait a minute, refresh once; it comes from public servers that can lag.

• Visitor stuck at "waiting" — they need my approval on the keeper's list.

• Google sign-in refuses on my site — Firebase console → Authentication → Settings → Authorized domains must include kladpay.github.io.

• Lost wallet — restore with the vault words (testnet).

• Anything else — ask Datastar Pro.
