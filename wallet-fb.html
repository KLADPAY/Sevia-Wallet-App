Leaving a custodial app?

In a custodial app, the company holds the keys. If it disappears, your access goes too. Here is how to move your bitcoin to your own wallet — slowly and safely.

Step 0 — Back up first. Make sure your 12 words are written on paper and tested. Never move more than you can afford to lose before your backup exists.
Step 1 — Get a receive address here. Open Receive in this wallet and copy the fresh address shown.
Step 2 — Send a small test amount. In your custodial app, withdraw a tiny amount first — the cost of a phone call, not your savings.
Step 3 — Verify it arrived. Watch this wallet until the test amount shows as confirmed. Paste the address, never retype it.
Step 4 — Move the rest. Only after the test lands, withdraw the full amount the same way.
Step 5 — You hold your own keys. Your money is now under your control alone. The old app cannot freeze it, and neither can anyone else.
Step 6 — Optional cleanup. Keep or close the old account. Your paper backup is now the thing to guard like cash.
Reminder: the old app verified your identity under its own rules. This wallet never asks for your ID and stores none of it.
